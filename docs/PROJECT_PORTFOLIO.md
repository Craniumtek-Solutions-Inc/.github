# Craniumtek Public Project Portfolio

This document is the public-safe portfolio map for Craniumtek Solutions Inc. It describes strategic areas at a high level and intentionally excludes confidential architecture, launch dates, financial plans, infrastructure details, customer data, credentials, and internal operating procedures.

## Status language

- **Active** — currently operated or actively maintained.
- **Incubation** — concept, planning, prototype, or development stage; not necessarily launched.
- **Frontier R&D** — long-horizon research direction requiring separate technical, safety, security, regulatory, and business-readiness gates.

## Industrial technology direction

These programs connect software to the physical economy:

- Cranium Manufacturing
- Cranium Factory OS
- Cranium Industrial AI
- Cranium Robotics
- Cranium Machine Intelligence / AI Direct-to-Machine
- Cranium Autonomous Systems
- Cranium Drones
- Cranium Mobility
- Cranium Electronics and Edge AI
- Cranium Energy Systems
- Cranium Materials
- Cranium FoodTech

The manufacturing strategy emphasizes learning from high-performing industrial ecosystems, rapid prototyping, supply-chain depth, design-for-manufacturing, automation, localization, original engineering, lawful intellectual-property practices, and eventual Philippine manufacturing capability.

## Frontier R&D direction

Long-horizon research may include:

- advanced robotics and autonomous machines;
- AI-to-machine control architectures with deterministic safety boundaries;
- embedded systems and future fabless semiconductor design;
- aerospace software, avionics, autonomous flight, and high-altitude systems;
- satellite software, ground systems, mission control, and CubeSat concepts such as CraniumSat;
- planetary robotics and remote autonomous manufacturing research;
- advanced materials and future space-manufacturing research.

Frontier initiatives are research directions, not claims of deployed capability.

## Shared foundation

Over time, operating ventures may share selected platform capabilities while retaining clear product and data ownership. Candidate shared capabilities include:

- Cranium ID and authentication;
- organization and role management;
- Cranium AI services;
- notifications and messaging primitives;
- search, maps, file/evidence, audit, and event abstractions;
- engineering standards and common SDK/contracts.

Shared infrastructure must not collapse independent products into one unrestricted database or trust boundary.

## Portfolio principle

Craniumtek aims to progress from software that assists people, to AI that coordinates work and businesses, to safe machine intelligence and industrial systems — while preserving human opportunity, accountability, security, and real-world usefulness.
