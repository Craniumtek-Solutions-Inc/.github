# Code of Conduct

Craniumtek Solutions Inc. wants technical collaboration to be professional, constructive, and safe.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, code, evidence, and outcomes;
- welcome good-faith questions and differing technical perspectives;
- give clear, actionable review feedback;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, intellectual property, and security boundaries;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, or deliberately disruptive behavior;
- disclose conflicts of interest when they materially affect a technical or business discussion.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory abuse, unwanted sexual attention, publication of private information, malicious security activity, or deliberate disruption of project spaces is not acceptable.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns that should not be handled publicly, contact **admin@craniumtek.com** with enough context for review.

Craniumtek maintainers may edit, remove, lock, or reject contributions and participation that violate these expectations or create material security, legal, or operational risk.
